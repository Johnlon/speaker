Visual interest — by shape class
"Square/non-round" means any non-circular face: square, rectangular, or squarish — the terms are interchangeable here. SQUARE ACCENT = round mid + non-round tweeter. BOTH SQUARE = non-round mid + non-round tweeter.
Non-round tweeters surveyed: MDT22T (Fs=650 Hz), MDT12 (Fs=1,000 Hz), DT-28N (Fs=1,200 Hz, 55×55mm), BC25SC55-04 (Fs=1,400 Hz), D26NC56-06 (Fs=1,625 Hz), SC 5 (Fs=2,000 Hz). MDT22T is the only one that achieves a viable crossover with any square-frame mid — every other non-round tweeter has Fs too high. ND140-4 (square, 138mm) has excellent SPL headroom but no compatible non-round tweeter — its beam ceiling (1,668 Hz) falls below the MDT22T's 1,800 Hz FR floor.
