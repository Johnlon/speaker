Visual interest — by shape class
"Square/non-round" means any non-circular face: square, rectangular, or squarish — the terms are interchangeable here. SQUARE ACCENT = round mid + non-round tweeter. BOTH SQUARE = non-round mid + non-round tweeter.
Non-round tweeters surveyed: MDT22T (Fs=650), MDT12 (Fs=1,000), DT-28N (Fs=1,200, 55×55mm), BC25SC55-04 (Fs=1,400), D26NC56-06 (Fs=1,625), SC 5 (Fs=2,000). Of these, MDT22T is the only one that achieves a viable crossover window with any square-frame mid — every other non-round tweeter has Fs too high. DT-28N (same 55mm format as MDT22T) is the closest alternative but fails at 3×Fs=3,600 Hz even against ND91-4's 2,816 Hz beam ceiling. ND140-4 (square, 138mm, +3.4 dB SPL headroom) pairs well with round tweeters (R2604: 0.34 oct tight at 1,484 Hz) but has no compatible non-round tweeter in catalogue.
