## Key Reference
- **Fs margin target:** Fs ≤75 Hz → 2× at 150 Hz crossover
- **Xmax ≥2.5 mm minimum; ≥4 mm preferred**
- **Sensitivity 82–90 dB workable** (outside range: >8 dB DSP correction needed)
- **Frame OD cap ~150 mm** for baffle width constraint (~190 mm baffle)
- Scanspeak model naming: **12W = 12 cm = 4.7"**, **18W = 18 cm = 7"** (too large)
