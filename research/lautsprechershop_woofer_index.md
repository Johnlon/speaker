## Summary

**Search filter used:** LSS parametric search, cutout 50–101mm, all driver types, June 2026. Prices inc 19% German VAT.

**Top picks within FP ≤ 122mm:**
1. **Wavecor WF110WA02** (4Ω, €46.20) — Xmax=3.5mm, 88.5dB, BR f3=71Hz implies low Fs. Best candidate pending T/S fetch.
2. **Monacor SPH-100C** (8Ω, €94.71) — Xmax=4mm, but 84dB sens needs DSP level trim vs sub. BR f3=45Hz suggests very low Fs.
3. **Wavecor WF090WA01** (4Ω, €68.20) — 90mm square, very compact, Xmax=2.75mm. No cabinet data; fetch T/S.

**SB Acoustics 4" family (FP=123mm, 1mm over):** Xmax=5mm across the range is exceptional. The NRX25 and NRXF25 variants at €57–59 are a strong argument for relaxing the FP limit by 1mm if baffle geometry allows.

**Wavecor WF110 vs WF090:** WF110 clearly better (Xmax+0.75mm, higher sens, cabinet data available). WF090 only wins if 90mm square footprint solves a tight spacing problem.

**Priority T/S fetches needed:** WF110WA02, WF090WA01, SPH-100C, SB12NRXF25-4.
